Hoarding Planner, a browser tool published as an artifact: https://claude.ai/artifact/J3jGFh4YS9PkZyyuJRK88z (built 2026-10-13). It has two tabs. On the shopfront photo tab you set a scale reference and draw red hoarding lines, a door (box with a cross), hatched areas, dimensions and text. The floor plan tab uses a mm grid with walls, hoarding, doors and dims. It exports a PNG with a title block (the title block is optional) and shows a schedule of hoarding run, face area, 2440x1220 board count and doors.

BSG's current manual style, from Gus's example (JustCuts photo): thin red lines drawn on the shopfront photo in perspective. The footprint goes from the shopfront corners out to a front line, and the hoarding door is a red rectangle with an X. The example has no dimensions.

**Why:** Gus had no tools and drew these by hand. **How to apply:** keep any changes consistent with that red-line style; the source file lives in the thread's scratchpad and must be republished to the same URL.

Gus's rules (2026-10-13): the red floor outline is the **hoarding limit**; the crossed box is the **hoarding entry**, and it must be aligned with the shop's own entrance (projected straight out from the entrance onto the front hoarding line).
